About OmniGEO

OmniGEO was founded with a singular conviction: Search is evolving from blue links to direct AI answers, and modern brands need transparency into how generative models perceive and cite them.

Our Core Philosophy: Pure BYOK (Bring Your Own Key)

Zero-Markup Inference Architecture

Unlike legacy SaaS platforms that charge 500% to 1000% markups on third-party API calls, OmniGEO operates as a pure software framework. You connect your own OpenRouter, OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google Gemini API key. All prompt evaluations execute directly from your client browser or edge workers at $0 platform inference markup.

Engineering & Privacy Guarantees

  • Client-Side Stored Keys: Your API keys are encrypted in your local browser storage and never stored on proprietary middleman servers.
  • Deterministic Multi-Model Grounding: Every citation position and brand mention is extracted and benchmarked across identical prompt temperatures.
  • Zero Spam / Zero Blackhat: We adhere strictly to ethical, white-hat Generative Engine Optimization practices (transparent robots.txt, structured llms.txt, and valid JSON-LD schemas).
