Schema.org JSON-LD Strategies for Generative AI Search Engines

Published by OmniGEO Research • 5 min read • Updated September 2026

Structured entity data provides large language models with unequivocal facts about your product features, pricing tiers, and answers to common comparison questions.

The Essential Schemas for AI Search Optimization

1. SoftwareApplication Schema

Tells AI crawlers your software category, supported operating systems, and official verified price tiers: 

<script type="application/ld+json">
{
  "@context": "https://schema.org",
  "@type": "SoftwareApplication",
  "name": "OmniGEO",
  "applicationCategory": "BusinessApplication",
  "operatingSystem": "Web",
  "url": "https://omnigeo.pages.dev",
  "offers": [
    {
      "@type": "Offer",
      "name": "Starter Framework",
      "price": "29.00",
      "priceCurrency": "USD"
    }
  ]
}
</script>

2. FAQPage Schema for Direct Answer Synthesis

AI engines like ChatGPT Search directly quote FAQPage markup when answering common buyer-intent queries about your product versus competitors.
